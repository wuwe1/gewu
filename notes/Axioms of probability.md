For each [[Event|event]] $E$, we denote $P(E)$ as the probability of event $E$ occurring.

*Axiom 1* ― Every probability is between 0 and 1 included, i.e:

$$
0\leqslant P(E)\leqslant 1
$$

![[probability-axiom-1.svg|633]]

*Axiom 2* ― The probability that at least one of the elementary events in the entire [[Sample space|sample space]] will occur is 1, i.e:

$$
P(S)=1
$$

*Axiom 3* ― For any sequence of mutually exclusive events $E_1, ..., E_n$, we have:

$$
P\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^nE_i\right)=\sum_{i=1}^nP(E_i)
$$
