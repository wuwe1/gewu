The bias of an [[Estimator|estimator]] $\hat{\theta}$ is defined as being the difference between the [[Expected value|expected value]] of the distribution of $\hat{\theta}$ and the true value, i.e.:

$$
\textrm{Bias}(\hat{\theta})=E[\hat{\theta}]-\theta
$$

Remark: an estimator is said to be unbiased when we have $E[\hat{\theta}]=\theta$.
