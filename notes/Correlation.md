By noting $\sigma_X, \sigma_Y$ the [[Standard deviation|standard deviations]] of $X$ and $Y$, we define the correlation between the random variables $X$ and $Y$, noted $\rho_{XY}$, as follows:

$$
\rho_{XY}=\frac{\sigma_{XY}^2}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}
$$

Remark 1: we note that for any random variables $X, Y$, we have $\rho_{XY}\in[-1,1]$.

Remark 2: If X and Y are [[Independence|independent]], then $\rho_{XY} = 0$.
