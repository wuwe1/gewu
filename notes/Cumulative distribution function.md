The cumulative distribution function $F$, which is monotonically non-decreasing and is such that $\underset{x\rightarrow-\infty}{\textrm{lim}}F(x)=0$ and $\underset{x\rightarrow+\infty}{\textrm{lim}}F(x)=1$, is defined as:

$$
F(x)=P(X\leqslant x)
$$

![[cdf.svg|633]]

Remark: we have $P(a < X\leqslant b)=F(b)-F(a)$.
