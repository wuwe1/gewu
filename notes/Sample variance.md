The sample variance of a [[Random sample|random sample]] is used to estimate the true [[Variance|variance]] $\sigma^2$ of a distribution, is often noted $s^2$ or $\hat{\sigma}^2$ and is defined as follows:

$$
s^2=\hat{\sigma}^2=\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\overline{X})^2
$$

Remark: the sample variance is [[Bias|unbiased]], i.e $E[s^2]=\sigma^2$.
