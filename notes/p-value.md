In a hypothesis test, the $p$-value is the probability under the null hypothesis of having a test statistic $T$ at least as extreme as the one that we observed $T_0$. We have:

$$
{\small\textrm{(left-sided)}}\quad p\textrm{-value}=P(T\leqslant T_0|H_0{\small\textrm{ true}})\quad\quad\quad{\small\textrm{(right-sided)}}\quad p\textrm{-value}=P(T\geqslant T_0|H_0{\small\textrm{ true}})
$$

$$
{\small\textrm{(two-sided)}}\quad p\textrm{-value}=P(|T|\geqslant |T_0||H_0{\small\textrm{ true}})
$$
