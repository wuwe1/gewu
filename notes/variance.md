# 方差和标准差

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=E\big[(X-E[X])^2\big]=E[X^2]-E[X]^2
$$

标准差：

$$
\sigma=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}
$$

- 连到：[[expected-value]]、[[independence]]、[[covariance]]、[[statistical-inference]]
